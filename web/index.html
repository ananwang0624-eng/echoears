How it works 它在做什么

Problem 问题

Two ICU-10201 transducers on the temples of a pair of glasses fire 175 kHz ultrasound — three octaves above human hearing — and record the echo profile out to ~2.5 m, 19 times a second. You cannot hear any of what they "see". · 镜腿上两颗超声传感器,人耳完全听不见。

Sonar mode 声呐(默认)

Distance becomes delay — the physics itself. A tick marks the emission; each echo returns after a delay proportional to its range, compressed in loudness, with reverb added. The real millisecond echo train is stretched tens of times into human time — the mirror of speeding seismograms up. · 滴一声后,回声按距离比例延迟返回,越远越晚。

Tones & sweep 音调与扫描

Tones: the strongest echoes each drive a continuous voice, pitch = log distance (near = high), sticky and hysteretic so voices glide instead of warbling. Sweep: the raw audification — one gated grain per frame, every bin voiced, kept as the honest control. · 音调:每个回波一条口哨;扫描:原始逐格,留作对照。

Why two ears 为什么双耳

Left temple to left ear, right to right — spatial hearing does the direction-finding for free, no training needed. The 2×2 matrix also carries two pitch-catch channels (one sensor fires, the other listens): ellipse loci with the sensors as foci, exposed in the mixer as a cross-check referee, off by default. · 左镜腿→左耳,右镜腿→右耳;交叉信道当裁判,默认关闭。

Signal path 信号通路

Static-clutter removal (offline median for recordings, streaming EMA live) → divide each range bin by its own measured noise → gate + blind zone → per-channel synthesis → mixer → lowpass → compressor. That middle step is why one gate works at every distance: raw echoes span ~70 dB across the range axis, and in noise-sigma units a static scene is flat to about 1 dB. The receiver opens only after the transmit burst, so the range axis itself starts ~16 cm out; the blind-zone slider trims ringdown residue just past that. Loudness is a fixed compressive curve, amp = (σ/8)^γ — there is no auto-gain left to chase, because per-bin normalisation removed the range spread it existed to hide. · 去静杂波→门限/盲区→合成→混音;RX 在发射后才开,轴从 ~16 cm 起。

Phase continuity 载波相位

Carrier phase is accumulated across grains and voices are slewed with setTargetAtTime — discontinuities in time are broadband clicks in frequency, so both are engineered out. · 相位连续累加,增益滑变,消除咔哒声。
